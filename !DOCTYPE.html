Her Name

The Meaning in Everything

They say names carry weight, holding fragments of a person’s soul. Iqra — “to read,” a seeker of depth and truth. Zaib — “beauty,” the quiet grace that adorns the world around her.

Together: the beautiful who reads. To me, it means the story I never want to end — the beauty that remains long after she has left the room.

“Time stops when I think of you. The world goes quiet, and it’s just the memory of your light.”
The Letters I Never Sent

Words Left Unspoken

I write letters to you in my mind before I fall asleep. I tell you about my days, the small things that reminded me of you — the quiet moments where your absence was the loudest sound in the room.

Every thought curves back to you. Every sentence ends with your name.

Your beauty remains.

Not just the way you look, though that alone could stop a heart. It’s the beauty of your spirit — the way you make the world softer just by being in it.

You are the warmth in the cold, the spark in the dark. A singular, radiant truth that I was lucky enough to witness.

Missing You

The Quiet Ache

There is a silence that fills the space where you used to be. It isn’t empty — it’s heavy, filled with the echoes of your laugh, the softness of your gaze, the warmth of your presence.

Missing you is like waiting for dawn in the middle of the night. The dark is beautiful, lit by the memory of you — but the longing for the sun remains.

Forever yours,

Endless Love
